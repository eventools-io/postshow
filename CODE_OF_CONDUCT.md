# Community code of conduct

Postshow welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work.
- Assume good intent while accepting clear technical correction.
- Give credit, respect licenses, and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect credentials, personal data, customer content, and private security
  information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and product safety without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, impersonation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable. Neither are attempts to use project spaces
to obtain credentials, customer data, or unauthorized access.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in this repository and in official Postshow community spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or restrict access when
behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@eventools.io](mailto:support@eventools.io) with the subject
`Postshow conduct report`. Do not include unrelated customer data or credentials.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical while allowing a fair review.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
